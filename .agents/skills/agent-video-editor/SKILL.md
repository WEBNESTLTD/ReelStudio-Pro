# Agent Video Editor — Professional Editing Judgment

## Core Editing Principles

### 1. Pacing & Retention Psychology
- **Vertical Reels (9:16)**:
  - First cut within 1.5 - 2.5 seconds.
  - Average shot length (ASL): 2.0 - 3.5 seconds.
  - Never hold a static frame without subtle motion (slow zoom, push-in, pan, or kinetic typography).
- **Cinematic Landscape (16:9)**:
  - Establish scenes with 4 - 6 second shots.
  - Cut on subject motion (match-cuts) to hide transitions.

### 2. Audio Level Standards
- **Dialogue / Voiceover**: -12 dB to -14 dB (clean, intelligible, no clipping).
- **Background Music**:
  - Standalone: -16 dB.
  - Ducked during dialogue: -24 dB to -28 dB (smooth 200ms ramp).
- **Sound Effects (Whooshes, Risers, Impacts)**:
  - -18 dB to -20 dB (punctuates visual motion without overpowering narration).

### 3. Visual Grading & Contrast
- High contrast and vibrant saturation perform best on mobile OLED/LCD displays.
- Apply subtle cinematic vignettes (`rgba(0,0,0,0.65)`) to focus viewer gaze on center subjects.
- Protect Instagram UI safe zones: avoid placing critical text or subjects in the bottom 250px or top 120px.
